01 / Biomedical

Understanding the body.
Engineering what helps.

My biomedical work spans computational modelling, medical technology, signal analysis, and simulation-based learning. Some projects are early-stage explorations; others are developed through coursework, research, or clinical exposure.

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT2025 — PRESENT

Selected work

A selection of ongoing and completed work, presented in its current scope.

01COMPUTATIONAL MODELLING

Skin roughness & melanoma thermography

Developing a 2D skin model in COMSOL to study how surface roughness may affect melanoma detection during infrared thermography.

02SIMULATION

3D skin capillary model

An individual MATLAB project developing a 3D skin capillary model for more realistic simulation studies related to skin cancer and melanoma detection.

03EXPLAINABLE AI

XAI for oral cancer diagnostics

Research internship project at UTM-IJN focused on explainable AI for oral cancer diagnostics, under the supervision of Prof. Madya Ir. Dr. Tan Tian Swee.

04CLINICAL ENGINEERING

CyberKnife & Tomotherapy

Clinical exposure at JPMC to CyberKnife S7, CyberKnife VSI, and Tomotherapy X9, including device operation, treatment procedures, error observation, and service-engineering workflows.

05VIRTUAL LEARNING

SimServ / SIM-IE

A biomedical engineering simulation and digital-twin learning ecosystem. The current direction includes virtual equipment environments and service-engineering training.

06BIOMEDICAL SIGNALS

P300 data analysis

Participated in the 2026 BCI hackathon, working on P300 speller data collected from five healthy subjects using an eight-channel Unicorn Brain Interface.

07INSTRUMENTATION

Basic hearing-aid circuit

Team-led design exploration applying amplifiers, filters, sound signals, and knowledge of ear anatomy to develop an economical sound-amplification and noise-reduction circuit.

08MOVEMENT ANALYSIS

Posture & gait deviations

Team project using Kinovea and statistical analysis to examine posture and gait deviations during bicep-curl exercise among gym trainees in Karachi.
